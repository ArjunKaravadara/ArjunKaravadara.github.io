✦ About me

a little about myself

I'm an independent 3D motion graphics designer from India. My work sits where design, animation, and code meet — turning a rough idea into something people can actually play with, use, or remember.

I like owning the whole process: shaping the visual direction, building the interaction, refining the 3D motion, and solving the practical details that make an experience feel finished.
